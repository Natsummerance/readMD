# OCR Pages Spread Review

## Verdict

The four localized OCR pages are suitable for staged publication. The review explains the resonance structure without predicting platform performance.

## Five-Theory Decode

1. **Silence spiral:** People rarely admit that they retype invoices, papers, contracts, or archive pages because their current tool uploads scans or produces unusable text. The pages name that friction plainly.
2. **Uses and gratifications:** Information value comes first; privacy and control follow. The reader learns where recognition runs, what to review, and why quality varies.
3. **Framing:** The page stands with the owner of a private or authoritative document, not with a one-off upload form. Local recognition is framed as preparation for careful editing.
4. **Two-step flow:** Likely first messengers are researchers, legal and finance staff, students, archivists, and support engineers who already teach colleagues how to digitize paper.
5. **Cognitive consistency:** The message confirms that scanned documents contain valuable structure, then challenges the habit of trusting raw OCR output without human review.

## Effective Stance

Stand with the person who must preserve evidence while moving from image to searchable text. Promise less retyping and a clear local boundary, not flawless recognition.
