## 2026-08-26 authentic capture quality repair

PASS after pausing publication. Pixel audits confirmed the user-reported defects in the prior captures: welcome pages had a 62.3% empty band, sharing panels about 40%, and the beta.3 presentation capture about 69.8%. The root cause was forcing every shot into one 960×1280 viewport and the same fixture; Reveal additionally rendered its 1080×720 stage at the top of a portrait iframe.

Fixes:

- Each shot now declares its own authentic viewport and document fixture. Reader/editor keep workbench proportions; presentation uses 16:9; welcome, picker, and sharing use desktop ratios without large dead bands.
- Seven distinct Markdown fixtures now drive different UI states instead of repeatedly showing one research file.
- Reveal.js, Markdown, highlight, notes, KaTeX, themes, fonts, and the presentation bootstrap are vendored locally. This removes CDN dependence and makes generated slides compatible with ReadMD's CSP.
- Slide sections now center content vertically, and capture targets the real Reveal stage rather than surrounding black space.
- Publisher QA now verifies raw screenshot dimensions against capture metadata and rejects near-blank images, long empty bands, and distorted frames before clicking.
- Pending feedback records participate in exact/near-duplicate originality checks even though their metrics remain excluded from engagement learning.
- Watcher JSON parsing ignores scalar lines so a publisher target ID cannot be mistaken for the publication result.
