# Answer Pages Spread Review

## Verdict

The two task guides are suitable for staged publication. They do not predict platform performance; the review below explains why the message has a resonant structure for technical writers, researchers, students, and support engineers.

## Five-Theory Decode

1. **Silence spiral:** Many users quietly avoid complaining that their "productivity" stack sends private documents through browser converters or rebuilds slides by hand. The pages say the local boundary plainly.
2. **Uses and gratifications:** The primary gratification is information value with a secondary control motive: keep the source authoritative, searchable, portable, and local.
3. **Framing:** The pages stand beside the person whose canonical work already lives in Markdown. They do not ask the reader to abandon that source for another tool's document model.
4. **Two-step flow:** Likely first movers are technical writers, researchers, course authors, and open-source maintainers who already answer these questions for colleagues.
5. **Cognitive consistency:** The pages confirm the belief that Markdown is durable and portable, then challenge the extra behavior of duplicating content into a separate deck before every talk.

## Effective Stance

Stand with the owner of a long-lived document, not with a one-off upload. The useful action is to preserve structure in place: paginate the view, repair only display, and present from the same source.
