# Conversion Pages Spread Review

## Verdict

The four localized conversion pages are suitable for staged publication. The review does not predict platform performance; it explains why the message has a durable resonance structure for people migrating inherited material into Markdown.

## Five-Theory Decode

1. **Silence spiral:** Users rarely say out loud that they retyped reports or uploaded private documents just to escape proprietary formats. The page names the local boundary without shaming the old workflow.
2. **Uses and gratifications:** Information value comes first; control and time recovery follow. The reader learns which formats are accepted, what structure survives, and where review is still required.
3. **Framing:** The page stands beside the owner of inherited work, not beside a tool that demands a clean start. Conversion becomes migration, not replacement.
4. **Two-step flow:** Likely first messengers are technical writers, researchers, course authors, analysts, and support engineers who already hand Markdown conventions to colleagues.
5. **Cognitive consistency:** The message confirms that existing documents contain useful structure, then challenges the habit of treating conversion as an all-or-nothing, upload-first task.

## Effective Stance

Stand with the person who must preserve evidence, formulas, tables, citations, and images while moving to a portable source. Promise less manual transfer, not perfect visual fidelity.
