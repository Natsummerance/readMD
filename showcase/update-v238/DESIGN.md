# ReadMD V2.3.8 editorial update deck

## Visual theme

`gc-minimal-zine-poster` interpreted as an editorial proof book: product evidence is treated as a specimen, with generous paper margins, restrained technical annotations and one deliberate ink colour. The deck is original; it borrows no source-layout, logo or copy from the supplied references.

## Palette and roles

- Paper: `#F2F0EA` — default background and breathing room.
- Ink: `#20231F` — all high-contrast primary text and rules.
- Moss: `#657555` — the only accent, used for registration marks and provenance lines.
- Blue-black: `#263F50` — an alternate technical-paper background, not a second accent.
- Bone: `#E6E1D5` — muted type and paper borders.

No gradients, pure black, neon or decorative colour noise are allowed.

## Typography

- Display: `Bodoni MT`, `Iowan Old Style`, `Baskerville`, `Noto Serif CJK SC`, serif.
- Information: `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, sans-serif.
- Evidence labels: `Cascadia Mono`, `SFMono-Regular`, monospace.

The display face creates the editorial hierarchy; Chinese body copy remains in a legible local sans font. Static output does not fetch remote fonts.

## Components

- Evidence frame: a full, uncropped authentic screenshot with a provenance label.
- Margin note: a short factual benefit with a hairline connector; it never overlays or obscures the screenshot.
- Registration block: page number, release range and source-verification status.
- Footer: compact scope statement, not a marketing CTA.

## Layout

Each page is a reproducible 1080×1440 portrait canvas. The source screenshot occupies at least 30% of the canvas, is never redrawn, masked as an illustration or repeated on another page. Four alternating compositions — specimen, ledger, blueprint and archive — prevent a card-grid rhythm while preserving a consistent reading path.

## Motion

The optional gallery uses only two low-amplitude treatments: the evidence frame fades upward on entry, and its provenance line draws from left to right. Exported PNGs are static and contain no motion-dependent information.

## Banned anti-patterns

No generic three-card grid, no bento layout, no glass effects, no fake metrics, no emoji, no duplicated evidence, no screenshot crop that removes a relevant feature, no text pasted over product controls, and no claim without a source file listed in the deck manifest.
