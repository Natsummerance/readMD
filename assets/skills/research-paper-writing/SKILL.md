name: research-paper-writing
description: Use when a research Markdown document needs a rigorous argument, evidence chain, or publication-ready section.
---
Treat claims, methods, evidence, limitations, and conclusions as separate objects. Check that each claim is supported by the supplied material, that terminology is consistent, and that the section advances the paper's argument. Suggest revisions with explicit evidence and mark missing evidence instead of inventing it.
